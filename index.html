Model: Fish populations grow according to a simplified logistic growth model. Fishing removes fish from the fished area only. When the MPA is active, fish inside it are protected from harvest; a small amount of movement between protected and fished waters represents spillover and migration.

This is a pedagogical model rather than a calibrated representation of a specific fishery. The numerical parameters are intentionally simplified so students can see population, carrying-capacity, fishing, refuge, and spillover dynamics.
